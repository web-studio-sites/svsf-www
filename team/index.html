Meet the Team

Vincent St Claire
Managing Director

Vincent has over 30 years’ experience in the automotive sector and is the MD of SVSF’s parent company Fleet Assist. He was previously MD of Arval, operations director at easyCar and commercial director of rental services at the AA.

Carl Blott
Financial Director

Carl joined Fleet Assist in 2018 as Finance Director and is responsible for overseeing the performance of the finance team, ensuring robust financial controls, and delivering accurate and timely company reporting. In his role, he plays a key part in supporting strategic decision-making through financial insight and analysis.

Sara Smith
Vehicle Supply and Funding Director

Sara has spent the last 29 years working within the automotive industry and heads up the SVSF business. Sara was previously group fleet and business sales manager for Stellantis Sales and Service.

Alex Young
Account Manager

Alex is an experienced Account Manager dedicated to building and maintaining strong client relationships and delivering tailored solutions that meet their business needs.

Emma Joel
Business Development Manager

Emma brings 29 years of expertise in Motability, fleet, B2B sales, and the rental market. She focuses on creating opportunities and expanding business.

Adam Hutchinson
Business Development Manager

Adam Hutchinson has joined the SVSF team as our new Business Development Manager, covering the Southern region.

Adam brings nearly 10 years’ experience across the automotive fleet, rental and leasing sectors, with a strong background in vehicle supply, contract hire and leasing, rental, fleet development and OEM relationships.

Having worked closely with leasing companies, brokers, rental operators and corporate fleets, Adam brings a great understanding of the market and, importantly, the needs of our customers.

Adam will be focused on developing new and existing customer relationships across the South and supporting the continued growth of SVSF.

Andria Fountain
Administration Manager

Andria has 36 years of experience across retail, fleet, and rental markets with leading mainstream brands. She leads the administration team with operational expertise and insight.

Katie Allsop
Administrator

Katie has over a decade of experience in the automotive industry, specialising in fleet management. She ensures smooth and efficient administrative support for the team.

Dawn Williamson
SVSF Administrator

Dawn brings over four years of experience within the automotive industry, supporting customers throughout the entire vehicle lifecycle.

Her expertise spans vehicle leasing proposals, sales order processing, vehicle delivery and service coordination, defleet management, account management, complaints resolution, and claims handling. With a strong customer-focused approach, Dawn works closely with clients to ensure a smooth and efficient experience, from vehicle delivery through to vehicle return. Her attention to detail, problem-solving skills, and commitment to delivering excellent service make her a valued member of the SVSF team.

Eamon Joyce
SVSF Administrator

Eamon brings over 40 years of experience within the automotive industry, having built an extensive career across a variety of dealership and commercial vehicle environments.

Since entering the motor trade in 1983, he has developed a wealth of expertise in sales administration, vehicle ordering, fleet administration, customer service, and operational support. Known for his strong organisational skills, attention to detail, and commitment to delivering exceptional customer service, Eamon plays a key role in ensuring the smooth and efficient management of vehicle orders.
