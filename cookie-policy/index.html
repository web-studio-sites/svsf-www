Cookie Policy

SVSF is a division of Fleet Assist Limited. Accordingly, any reference in this Policy to ‘Fleet Assist Limited’ or ‘Fleet Assist’ shall be deemed to include SVSF.

Our website uses cookies to distinguish you from other users of our website. This helps us to provide you with a good experience when you browse our website and also allows us to improve our site.

A cookie is a small file of letters and numbers that we store on your browser or the hard drive of your computer if you agree. Cookies contain information that is transferred to your computer’s hard drive.

We use the following cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies. Necessary cookies are required to enable the basic features of this site, such as providing secure log-in or adjusting your consent preferences. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable data.
  • Analytical cookies. Analytical cookies are used to understand how visitors interact with the website. These cookies help provide information on metrics such as the number of visitors, bounce rate, traffic source, etc.

We do not use advertisement, performance or functional cookies.

You can find more information about the individual cookies we use and the purposes for which we use them below:

  • Cookie name: Cookieyes-consent
  • Duration: 1 year
  • Description: This is for the purpose of remembering a user’s consent preferences so that their preferences are respected on subsequent visits to this site. It does not collect or store any personal information about the site visitors.

Analytical cookies

  • Cookie name: _ga_*
  • Duration: 1 year 1 month and 4 days
  • Description: Google Analytics sets this cookie to store and count page views.

Third Party Cookies

The only third party who use cookies via our website are Google Analytics, over which we have no control. These third-party cookies are analytical cookies.

To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, visit http://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Choices about cookies

You will have been asked when you first visited our website to consent to our use of cookies, or in the alternative to reject such cookies or provide customised consent. You can manage your cookie preferences (save for strictly necessary cookies) via our website (specifically via the cookie symbol in the bottom left-hand corner of our website). You can choose which analytical cookies we can set by clicking on the relevant button(s).

You can also choose to “Reject All” cookies in the cookie banner.

However, if you use your browser settings to block all cookies (including essential cookies) you may not be able to access all or parts of our website.

If you have any questions or concerns about our use of cookies, please send us an email at customer.services@fleetassist.co.uk.
