Cookie Policy

SVSF is a division of Fleet Assist Limited. Accordingly, any reference in this Policy to ‘Fleet Assist Limited’ or ‘Fleet Assist’ shall be deemed to include SVSF.

A cookie is a small file of letters and numbers that a website stores on your browser or the hard drive of your computer. Cookies contain information that is transferred to your computer’s hard drive.

Our website only uses cookies if you agree. With your permission, we use analytical cookies to understand how visitors use our website. This helps us to improve our site.

We do not use advertising, performance or functional cookies, and we do not set any cookies until you accept them.

Your choice

When you first visit our website, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Nothing that sets cookies is loaded until you choose:

  • If you click Accept, we use Google Analytics, which sets the analytical cookies described below.
  • If you click Reject, no cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you change from Accept to Reject, we remove the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

To remember your choice, your browser keeps a small note of it on your device (in your browser’s local storage, not in a cookie). It contains only the word “granted” or “denied”, is never sent to us, and stays until you change your choice or clear your browser’s data.

Analytical cookies

These are only set if you click Accept:

  • Cookie name: _ga
  • Duration: 2 years
  • Description: Google Analytics sets this cookie to tell visitors apart, by assigning a randomly generated number. It lets us see how many people visit our website and how they use it, without identifying you personally.
  • Cookie name: _ga_* (followed by a code for our website)
  • Duration: 2 years
  • Description: Google Analytics sets this cookie to keep track of your visit, such as which pages you view.

Third party cookies

The only third party whose cookies may be used on our website is Google, for Google Analytics, and only if you accept them. Google’s use of this information is covered by its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, visit https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

The map on our Contact Us page is provided by OpenStreetMap. It does not set any cookies.

Choices about cookies

You can also use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. If you do, our website will still work, because it does not need cookies to function.

If you have any questions or concerns about our use of cookies, please send us an email at customer.services@fleetassist.co.uk.
